# EduBloom documentation (from researcher's screenshot of the Kaggle data card, captured 2026-11-19)
Title: "EduBloom: Bloom Taxonomy Questions Dataset"; uploader Pushkar Sharma; licence CC BY-SA 4.0; usability 9.41; update frequency annual.
The card describes 73,000+ questions labelled by Bloom level, subject, topic, question type and difficulty (Easy/Medium/Hard), intended for
educational AI/NLP research, and recommends additional human and subject-expert validation for real-world or high-stakes use.
The visible part of the card does NOT state how questions or labels were produced. Our audit (Phase 58/64) shows labels are not recoverable
from text beyond the difficulty field. CC BY-SA 4.0 requires attribution and share-alike for redistributed derivatives.
